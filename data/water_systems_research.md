# Erie County

[The Erie County Water System](https://www.eriecounty.oh.gov/WaterMainsServices.aspx) provides safe drinking water to over 9,000 homes and businesses throughout the County. All of the water that is distributed is purchased from the cities of Sandusky, Huron, Vermilion and Northern Ohio Rural Water, which have their own facilities for treating water from Lake Erie. Erie County then distributes the water through a network of 12 pump stations, 11 water towers, 1,000 valves and 200 miles of 4-inch to 16-inch water mains.
